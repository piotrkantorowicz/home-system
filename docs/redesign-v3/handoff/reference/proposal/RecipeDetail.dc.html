Method

  1. 1Rinse the quinoa, then simmer in twice its volume of water for 12–15 minutes. Leave covered for 5 minutes.
  2. 2Season the chicken and fry in the olive oil over medium-high heat, 5–6 minutes a side. Rest for 3 minutes, then slice.
  3. 3Halve the tomatoes and dice the cucumber.
  4. 4Spoon the quinoa into a bowl, arrange the chicken and vegetables on top and finish with the hummus.
